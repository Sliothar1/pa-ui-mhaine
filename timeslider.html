Uí Mhaine · Hy Many

Uí Mhaine through time

Twelve stops, from Máine Mór to the end of the old order. Watch the kingdom grow and settle along the Suck and the Shannon.

Approximate / interpretation. The shaded shapes are sketches of how far the kingdom reached in each era, drawn from Curley's account of a shifting boundary. They are not surveyed lines. The corrected boundary (Garry Lohan, 12 Oct 2026) will be added as its own layer.

Keyboard: ← and → (or , and .) step between stops, Home and End jump to first and last; the slider also works with arrow keys. Animation is reduced if your device asks for less motion.

Layers

  • O'Donovan 1843, shown faintly: his map is treated as a claim, not a fact.
  • Routes: the Slighe Mhór along the Eiscir Riada, and the Suck and Shannon (indicative lines), and the poets' route to Pallas (route as told locally).
  • Assembly sites, such as Tullyneeny (location to be confirmed).
  • Learned families and the court: Lios Mór at Pallas, Doon and Ballynabanaba.
