Overview

An immersive, fact-led room in Fohenagh, Co. Galway. Visitors fly over the landscape of Uí Mhaine as it changes through the centuries, from ringforts and eskers to the 1351 Christmas feast, Kilconnell Friary, Knockdoe and Aughrim, and on to today. The aim is awe and reverence, not pomp, with this place seen in its European context.

Working draft. Every claim names its source; anything marked “verify” is still unconfirmed. Only public sites are mapped. Private farmland is deliberately left out.
